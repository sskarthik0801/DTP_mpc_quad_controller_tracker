Access token needed

It is printed by bash scripts/gcs.sh at startup, and stored on the Jetson in ~/.cache/super-gcs/token. This browser remembers it, so you only paste it once — after that http://gypsy.local:8080 is enough on its own.

Admin control

Separate from the dashboard token, and narrower: the token says you may fly this aircraft, this says you may use the restricted panels. It is remembered for this browser tab only and is forgotten when the tab closes, when the GCS restarts, or after 30 minutes with the tab in the background. Change it with python3 scripts/gcs/admin.py --set.
