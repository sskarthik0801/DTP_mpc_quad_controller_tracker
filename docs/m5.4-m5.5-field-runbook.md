## 1. M5.4a — outdoor depth (plan row 4)

**Decides:** does the Gemini 336L's active stereo still range a person in sunlight?
- **Yes:** camera depth stays the default.
- **No:** the LiDAR depth path becomes mandatory, and every remount costs a recalibration.

```bash
# camera driver only, WITH the IR pair -- NOT the follower (the test runs its own YOLO)
ros2 launch dtp_bringup sensors.launch.py lidar:=false enable_ir:=true
python3 drone-dtp/scripts/outdoor_depth_test.py --minutes 12      # writes ~/dtp_datasets/<date>_outdoor_depth
```

**`enable_ir:=true` is not optional.** Without the IR pair the test's sun/shade classification
silently does not run, and a bad depth result has no cause attached. It costs depth rate
(30 → ~20 Hz), not validity.

**Output goes to `~/dtp_datasets/<date>_outdoor_depth`** since 2026-09-13. The old default was
`/tmp/outdoor`, which is cleared on reboot.

**Walk it:** the subject stands at **2, 4 and 6 m**, in **sun, shade, and under canopy**, facing and
side-on. The script detaches and survives SSH dropping.

**Pass (plan §2.2 M5.4):** on-person depth return **≥ 50 % of box pixels at 4 m in direct sun**.

## 2. M5.4b — motor-on calibration, from the Stage A flight bag (plan row 18)

**Why not a props-on ground capture** (`recalib.sh capture`), which is what the calibration notes
describe:
- PX4 disarms **10 s** after an arming with no takeoff (`COM_DISARM_PRFLT`), and capture dwells
  15 s per viewpoint.
- Capture needs the LiDAR on PointCloud2 (`xfer_format 0`). That starves FAST-LIO, the
  configuration that sent it 3.74 km off on 09-12.
- Ground-idle vibration is not hover vibration.

**Instead:** `drone-dtp/scripts/bag_to_calib_pairs.py` turns a Stage A bag into `score_alignment`'s
capture format.
- Each colour frame is paired with the LiDAR sweeps around it.
- Those sweeps are motion-compensated through FAST-LIO's own poses into the frame's instant.
- The unchanged scorer then runs on hover data:

```bash
python3 drone-dtp/scripts/bag_to_calib_pairs.py ~/dtp_datasets/<date>_stageA --out ~/calib_runs/<date>_flight
ros2 run dtp_perception score_alignment --run_dir ~/calib_runs/<date>_flight
```

**Pass (plan §2.2 M5.4):** motor-on `|Δu|, |Δv| ≤ 2 px` (bench: 0 / +1).

**The converter must reproduce a known motors-off score first** — on a bench bag of the same mount
(§4 has that run). Otherwise a flight "shift" could be the converter's, not the mount's.

## 3. M5.5 — Stage A in the air (plan row 16)

**What Stage A is.** The aircraft flies **its own mission**: the pilot in Position, or a click goal
on the click config. The follow stack runs observe-only, and every goal and yaw target it computes
goes to `/dtp/shadow/*`. It answers the one question a bench cannot: **do the goals degrade with
ego-motion, above all with yaw?**

**The sequence is `drone-dtp/scripts/field.sh`** (prep → arm → watch → land), unchanged. What is new
since 09-12:
- **`stage_a_preflight.py` reads the SUPER config off the running `/fsm_node`**, and **FAILs if the
  M5.3a target filter is on.** In Stage A the walker must be an obstacle to the mission's planner.
- **The 09-12 bags do not count.** One ran on a 3.74 km-diverged FAST-LIO, and all of them ran while
  ARK's logloader was looping.

**Fly it so the yaw table fills.** Section 3 of the analyzer needs yaw-rate spread, and S3 below
refuses to fit a slope over less than 0.3 rad/s.
- Hover at 1.0–1.5 m.
- Yaw slowly left and right through ±90°, at ~0.2, ~0.5 and ~0.8 rad/s.
- The walker walks slow left–right lines at **4–6 m** throughout.
- Three sorties of ~5 min beat one of 15.

**Score:**

```bash
python3 drone-dtp/scripts/analyze_shadow_goals.py ~/dtp_datasets/<date>_stageA     # section 6 = the M5.5 verdict
```

| Code | Threshold (plan §2.2 M5.5) | Measured as |
|---|---|---|
| S1 | standoff-goal error p95 ≤ 0.5 m | distance of each shadow goal **outside** the planner's legal band [2.75, 4.0] m. **Not** \|standoff − 3.5\|: the lead legitimately spends up to 0.75 m at walking pace |
| S2 | lifted-target jitter p95 ≤ 0.3 m | residual from a centred 5-sample median of `/dtp/follow_target` |
| S3 | error slope vs yaw rate ≤ 0.1 m per rad/s up to 0.8 rad/s | least-squares slope of **jitter** against yaw rate (the A8 question). Refused if the yaw rate spans < 0.3 rad/s |
| S4 | perception blind ≤ 3 % | fraction of `/dtp/perception_ok` False |

**Exit codes:**
- **0** — M5.5 passes;
- **1** — a threshold failed;
- **3** — INCOMPLETE: not scorable, or not a Stage A bag. **Not a pass.**

**From the same bag, also run:**

- **P4**, the frame-matched latency check ([`fact-fastlio-latency.md`](fact-fastlio-latency.md)),
  which has never seen a moving rig.
