## 1. The geometry that decides whether the day is worth anything

The oracle casts 15 rays: 5 lateral offsets across ±0.25 m at **3 heights, `sample_dz`
−0.5 / 0 / +0.4 m about the reported waist point**. Two floors delete heights before any ray is
cast, and a deleted height is not evidence:

- **the map's virtual ground** — `rog_map/virtual_ground_height: -1.0`, i.e. 1 m below
  `lidar_init`'s origin. Everything under it reads OCCUPIED.
- **the sensor's view floor** — the MID360 looks no lower than **7°** under its horizon, so at
  range *d* the map knows nothing below `z_sensor − d·tan 7°`, for ever.

**Consequence 1 — start FAST-LIO with the rig ON THE GROUND, then lift it.** Start it at
1.5 m and `lidar_init`'s origin is 1.5 m up, the virtual ground is at 0.5 m *above the real
ground*, and the subject's legs and waist are below it. That is exactly what void'd run5.

**Consequence 2 — carry height and subject range together decide how many of the three rows
survive.** `tan 7° = 0.1228`. With the subject's waist ≈ 1.0 m above ground (sample rows at
**0.5 / 1.0 / 1.4 m**):

| carry height | view floor at 3 m | at 4 m | at 5 m | rows that survive at 4 m |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1.5 m (the plan's number) | 1.13 m | 1.01 m | 0.89 m | **the top row only** → `rays_cast` 5 |
| **1.2 m (recommended)** | 0.83 m | 0.71 m | 0.59 m | middle + top → `rays_cast` 10 |
| 1.0 m | 0.63 m | 0.51 m | 0.39 m | middle + top, bottom marginal |

**Deviation from the plan, deliberate: carry at ~1.2 m, not 1.5 m.** 1.2 m is also the follow
height the aircraft actually flies (`dtp_params.yaml`, since 2026-09-16), so it is the more
honest rehearsal as well as the one that leaves two rows of evidence. The plan's 1.5 m collapses
the classifier onto one row at exactly the 4 m range the whole study is about.

**Consequence 3 — the occluder must rise above that floor at its own range.** A 1.8 m tube or a
tree trunk does. A chest-high cabinet, a car bonnet, a hedge below 1.2 m does not: the map has no
cells where the rays go, and the honest verdict is LOST, not OCCLUDED. **This is a precondition
of the paper's claim, not a tuning knob.**

**In the field, `rays_cast` tells you all of this in one number** (§7). 15 = three rows,
10 = two, 5 = one. If you are seeing 5, lower the rig or move the subject further out before
recording a single scripted event.

## 3. The course

- **8 occluders, ≥ 1.8 m tall, ~0.3 m wide**, two staggered rows 2.5 m apart, 2.0 m between
  occluders. Cardboard tubes are the plan's choice; wheelie bins, road-work poles, fence posts
  with a board, or **real tree trunks** all work — the only requirement is §1's height.
- **Record every occluder position to ±5 cm** (tape from two fixed corners is enough). The
  reappearance numbers in M6.4's exit criteria need the true edge, not the mapped one.
- A **tripod filming the whole course**, and a **clap** at the start of recording to sync it.
  Video is the ground truth; the contact sheets are an aid, not the label (run5's labels came
  from sheets, by Claude, and are flagged provisional in the paper for exactly that reason).
- Finish somewhere with **real trees, leaves on**, for one segment.

## 4. Bring-up, in this order

Rig powered, **disarmed, props off, sitting on the ground**:

# shell 2 -- the follower, observe-only. Goals go to /dtp/shadow/*; it commands nothing.

Let FAST-LIO settle (1–3 min) **with the rig still on the ground**, confirm odom is 10 Hz and
its stamps are fresh, then lift the rig to 1.2 m and keep it there.

```bash

```

**Nobody subscribes a laptop to `/cloud_registered`.** It is a field rule and it has already cost
one day: a Wi-Fi RViz blocked the publisher and every stamp went 38 s stale while the rate check
still passed.

**This bring-up was rehearsed on the bench 2026-09-23** with `bench_bringup.sh`
(`~/dtp_datasets/20260923_1656_bench_bringup/`, exit 0, 0 survivors, swap 0 MB). What it showed,
all of which you should expect to see again tomorrow:

- odom 10.00 Hz, stamp lag p50 19 / max 29 ms; GPU cloud 12–14 ms `[cuda]`; `/dtp/targets`
  7.8 Hz; no foreign subscribers on `/cloud_registered` or `/livox/lidar`.
- **`estimator_preflight` GO** (12/12) and **`stage_a_preflight` GO with 4 warnings.** All four
  are expected and none blocks this day: no `goal_mode` on `follow_planner_node` (the nmpc build
  is not installed — correct), `auto_reacquire_after_lost` true, `extrinsic_est_en` true, and no
  recorder running yet.
- **The oracle answered live at 25.9 % of a core** (22.5 % idle), RSS +1 MB, eval p99 161 µs.
- **`rays 10`, not 15** — the rig was low on the bench and the view floor dropped one height row,
  exactly the §1 arithmetic, on real hardware. This is the confirmation that `rays_cast` is a
  working field instrument.

Two of those warnings are worth a thought on a **carried** rig, neither a blocker:

- **`extrinsic_est_en: true`.** The oracle's ray origin is the body pose plus
  `camera_offset_body`, so it rides on FAST-LIO's online LiDAR↔IMU extrinsic. That estimate is
  well-conditioned when the rig is moving, which carrying it is — but if a scene's verdicts look
  systematically biased to one side, this is the first thing to suspect.
- **`auto_reacquire_after_lost: true`.** It changes how `target_manager` ends a coast, so it
  shapes the *episodes* you record. It does not affect the oracle's verdicts, and the scoring in
  §9 replays through a fresh `target_manager` anyway, so leave it alone.

Note that `bench_bringup.sh` also starts both PX4 bridges. **§4 above deliberately does not** —
nothing flies on this day, and the offboard bridge in IDLE publishes `trajectory_setpoint` at
50 Hz, which corrupts Altitude and Position mode (CLAUDE.md, 2026-09-21). Do not start the
bridges for a handheld session.

## 5. Recording

Depth is 6 MB/s and this study does not use it, so record an explicit list rather than the
`stage_b` preset:

```bash
cd ~/dtp_datasets
ros2 bag record -o $(date +%Y%m%d_%H%M)_m63_handheld \
  /cloud_registered_body /lidar_slam/odom /tf /tf_static /livox/imu \

  /rog_map/target_exclusion /planning/loss_event \
  /camera/color/image_raw/compressed /camera/color/camera_info
```

≈ 5 MB/s → ~12 GB for 40 min. `/cloud_registered_body` + `/lidar_slam/odom` are what
`loss_replay.py` rebuilds the map from; the compressed colour is what `loss_label.py` makes
sheets from; `/planning/loss_event` is the live verdict stream, which the replay does **not**
need but which is the only record of what the oracle said *on the aircraft's own clock*.

**Note the PID and stop it with `kill -INT <pid>`.** Never `pkill -f`, never read the `.db3`
while it is writing (that killed the 2026-09-13 bench bag), and **`sync` before pulling power**
(that cut the 2026-09-21 flight bag).

## 6. The event script

Call each event aloud — the number and the letter — before you start walking it. The subject
walks; the rig is carried behind at 4–5 m, level, nose on the subject.

| | event | ground truth | plan n | a realistic first day |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **E1** | walk **through** an occluder's shadow, left→right and right→left | OCCLUDED | 60 | 30 |
| **E2** | walk away in clear view past 10 m until the detector drops them | LOST | 40 | 20 |
| **E3** | leave the ±35° camera cone sideways | OUT_OF_VIEW | 20 | 10 |
| **E4** | crouch, or turn fully away, in clear view | LOST (detector FN) | 20 | 10 |

≈ 40 s an event. The full 140 is ~95 min of recording; 70 events is ~45 min and still gives
usable Wilson bounds on E1 and E2.

**The one thing E1 has to get right: walk THROUGH the shadow, do not stop at its edge.** Both
existing bags miss OCCLUDED for the same reason — the last seen position is beside the occluder,
and the CTRV point at coast onset has not entered the shadow yet, so the ray is genuinely clear
and LOST is the correct answer. The event must put the *coasted* point behind the occluder, which
means the subject keeps walking at a steady pace into and out of it.

**Vary, and write down:** the occluder (tube vs trunk), the subject's range (3 / 4 / 5 m),
walking speed, and the crossing direction. Those are the covariates the sweeps in §10 need.

## 7. What to watch while recording

Third shell:

```bash
ros2 topic echo /planning/loss_event --field rays_cast    # 15 / 10 / 5 -- see SS1
ros2 topic echo /planning/loss_event --field cls          # 0=OCCLUDED 1=UNMAPPED 2=LOST 3=OUT_OF_VIEW
```

and in shell 1, `fsm_node`'s own line every 5 s:
`-- [LossOracle] N events (...) classify p50/p99, total p50/p99`.

**Abort and fix, do not record around, if you see:**
- `target z ... is BELOW the map's virtual ground` — FAST-LIO was started at height. Restart the
  stack on the ground. The session is void until you do.
- `rays_cast` stuck at 5 — §1. Lower the rig or lengthen the range.
- E1 events coming back `cls 1` (UNMAPPED) rather than 0 — the occluder is being *seen* as
  unknown space rather than occupied. Usually means the LiDAR has not swept that bearing yet;
  walk the rig round the course once before recording so every occluder is mapped from two
  sides. (A trunk mapped from one side is a wall, not a shell —
  [`m6-loss-oracle.md`](m6-loss-oracle.md) "what B can and cannot do".)

## 9. Scoring — same evening

**The whole chain was verified end to end on 2026-09-23** on a 60 s slice of run5: replay →
26 events in 12 episodes → 12 contact sheets → `labels.csv`, and the scorer reproduced the
documented run5 confusion matrix exactly (5/1/32 and 2/2/4, E1 0.13, E2 0.25). It works on
today's binary; nothing here is untested.

**On the NX** (needs the bag, so it runs here):

```bash
source ~/super_ws/install/setup.bash
BAG=~/dtp_datasets/<stamp>_m63_handheld

# bag -> the REAL fsm_node -> events.jsonl + episodes.csv   (domain 87, one at a time)
python3 drone-dtp/scripts/loss_replay.py $BAG --out $BAG/loss_replay

# contact sheets -- NO --gui here, see below
python3 drone-dtp/scripts/loss_label.py sheets $BAG $BAG/loss_replay/events.jsonl \
        --out $BAG/loss_replay/label_sheets
```

**⚠️ `--gui` will not work on the NX over SSH: `DISPLAY` is unset** (checked 2026-09-23; `cv2`
and `numpy` are both installed, so sheet *generation* is fine — it is only the one-keypress
labelling that needs a display). Two ways to label:

- `ssh -X` into the NX and re-run the `sheets` command with `--gui`; or
- copy the sheet directory to the laptop and **fill `labels.csv` by hand** — it already has one
  row per episode with `oracle_first`, `oracle_majority`, `recorded_outcome` and the sheet
  filename, and an empty `label` column. Put `OCCLUDED` / `LOST` / `OUT_OF_VIEW` / `UNMAPPED`
  in it.

With `--gui`: one keypress per sheet — `o` OCCLUDED, `l` LOST, `v` OUT_OF_VIEW, `u` UNMAPPED,
`s` skip/unsure, `q` quit keeping what you have.

Each sheet is twelve thumbnails spanning ±2 s with the onset frame boxed in red, and a header
giving the oracle's verdict, its blocked/unknown fractions, its majority verdict over the coast,
and what the recorded `target_manager` did next. Then, wherever you labelled:

```bash
# the D1 numbers
python3 drone-dtp/scripts/loss_label.py score \
        $BAG/loss_replay/label_sheets/labels.csv $BAG/loss_replay/events.jsonl
```

**Label from the tripod video with the sheets beside it, not from the sheets alone.** The
event you called aloud is the label; the sheet only locates it in time. The onset frame is one
detection period *after* the last frame the person was seen in, so the frame that explains the
loss is the marked one and the two to its left.

`loss_replay.py` has `--filter on|off` (the M5.3a target-exclusion arm) and `--virtual-ground`
(for bags already recorded the wrong way — you should not need it if §4 was followed).

## 11. Pass lines (plan §2.2)

| | threshold |
|---|---|
| **D1** E1 OCCLUDED recall | **≥ 0.85** (n 60 → 95 % Wilson lower bound ≈ 0.74) |
| **D1** E2 false-OCCLUDED | **≤ 0.10** (n 40 → upper bound ≈ 0.23) |
| **D1** E3 OUT_OF_VIEW correct | **≥ 0.90** |
| **D1** timing | classify p99 ≤ 2 ms, evaluation p99 ≤ 10 ms (bench 2026-09-23: **111 µs / 2.47 ms**) |
| **M6.4** reappearance, on E1 | correct edge side ≥ 80 %; median \|t_pred − t_obs\| ≤ 0.5 s; median position error ≤ 0.5 m; the 90 %-mass region contains the true re-emergence ≥ 80 % |

**D1 failing means the classifier gets fixed before any M6 flight.** That is the point of doing
this on foot.

**Do not launch with `loss_conditioning:=true` on a day with flight authority.**
`stage_a_preflight.py --stage-b` FAILs on it, correctly: the conditioned arm has never seen a
real occlusion, and this day is what would first give it one. Recording with it **off** costs
nothing — §9 recovers both arms from the same bag.

## 12. Traps, all of them already paid for

- **FAST-LIO started at height voids the day** (run5). On the ground, then lift.
- **An occluder below the sensor's view floor at its range is not classifiable** (run5's cabinet,
  Stage A's grass). §1.
- **Stopping at the shadow's edge records a LOST and labels it OCCLUDED.** §6.
- **One gate or replay at a time**, whatever the domain ID. 87 = the loss replay tools,
  88 = `loss_oracle_check`.
- **Never `pgrep -f` / `pkill -f` a pattern that is in your own shell's command line** — it has
  killed a shell and deadlocked a wait loop in this repo. Match an argv basename, or wait on a PID
  (`tail --pid=<pid> -f /dev/null`).
- **Never read the bag while it records.** `sync` before power off.
- **No laptop on `/cloud_registered`.**
- **A second Livox driver** is how FAST-LIO went 3.74 km off. `run.sh` starts the only one;
  `dtp_follower.sh` refuses to start another.
